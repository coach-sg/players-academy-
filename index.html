Privacy policy

Effective October 9, 2026

SG Tennis Academy ("we", "us") runs junior tennis programs in Houston, Texas. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and our programs, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

From parents and guardians: your name, email address, phone number, and messages you send us.

About players: the player's name, age, skill level, session bookings, and coach notes on progress and goals.

Payments: purchase history and credit balances. Card details are handled by our payment processor and are not stored by us.

How we use it

To run sessions and bookings, send schedule changes and weather cancellations, track player progress, process payments, and answer your questions. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Texts and emails

We only send session updates by text or email if you opt in. Reply STOP to any text or UNSUBSCRIBE to any email to opt out. Message and data rates may apply. Mobile numbers and text opt-in consent are never sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing.

Children's information

Our players are minors. We collect player information from a parent or guardian, not directly from children. A parent or guardian can review, correct, or ask us to delete their child's information at any time.

Who we share it with

Our coaches, and service providers that help us run the academy, such as hosting, email, text messaging, and payment processing. They may use the information only to provide those services. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Keeping and protecting it

We keep information while your family is enrolled and for a reasonable period afterward for records and billing. Access is limited to academy staff who need it.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with an updated date. Questions or requests: coach@thesgacademy.com.
